Who we are

This site, bestshowerkit.com, is operated by Best Shower Kit, a brand of Aging Safely Baths. When this policy says “we”, it means us. You can reach us at 1-888-779-2284.

What we collect

When you use the quote form, we receive what you type: your name, phone number, email address, and anything else you choose to add, such as your ZIP code, the size of your shower opening, what you need, whether you want installation, and your message. The form also sends the page you sent it from and the time the page was loaded. It includes a hidden field designed to catch automated spam; people never see or fill it.

When you call us, we note what you tell us so we can prepare a quote and follow up on the questions you asked.

We do not ask for payment details, Social Security numbers or health records through this site. Please do not send them through the form.

How we use it

  • To answer your question and prepare the quote you asked for.
  • To follow up on that inquiry by phone or email.
  • To keep a record of what we quoted, so we can honor it if you come back.

Your details are not sold, rented or swapped with anyone, and they never go onto a mailing list you did not request.

Services this site uses

Form delivery. Quote requests are sent through FormSubmit, a third-party form service that delivers the message to our inbox by email. Their handling of the message is governed by their own terms.

Fonts. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests font files from Google’s servers. Google describes how it handles those requests in its Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

BBB seal. The Better Business Bureau seal image is loaded from the BBB’s servers, and clicking it opens the BBB website.

This site does not use analytics tools, advertising pixels or tracking cookies, and it does not store anything in your browser.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and quote records for as long as they are useful for answering you and honoring quotes, and for our ordinary business records. If you would like your inquiry erased, say so and it will be, unless we need to keep a record of a completed order.

Your choices

  • You can call instead of using the form.
  • Phone us to find out what is on file for you, to have it fixed, or to have it removed: 1-888-779-2284.
  • One call or email from you and we stop getting in touch.

Children

This site is meant for adults planning a bathroom project, and it is not designed to gather details from anyone under 18.
