What to send for the fastest quote

  • Opening width and depth, and whether the old walls are still on. Our measuring guide has a worksheet.
  • Where the drain is: left, right or center, and the distance from the back wall.
  • The floor: concrete slab, or wood framing with a basement or crawlspace below.
  • Who will use the shower, and whether a walker, shower chair, wheelchair or helper is involved.
  • What you need: pan only, pan and walls, or a full package with accessories.
  • Installation: yours, your contractor’s, or an installed price from us.
  • Your ZIP code, so we can quote freight.

What happens next

  1. We read what you sent and call or email if we have questions.
  2. You get a written quote showing the product, the freight and, if you asked, installation as separate lines.
  3. You decide in your own time. Compare it, ask follow-up questions, and call back when you are ready. Prices can change with materials and freight.
