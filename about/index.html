Who runs this site

Best Shower Kit is a brand of Aging Safely Baths, the Denver, Colorado business that has been making bathrooms safer and easier to use since 2012. Its crews fit walk-in tubs and roll-in showers, frequently finishing in one day; customers come from every one of the 50 states, and orders can go supply-only to Canada. Best Shower Kit is the part of the business that focuses on shower kits: the bases, the wall systems and the accessories, sold on their own or installed.

Aging Safely Baths is a BBB Accredited Business. You can read the BBB profile yourself; we would rather you check than take our word for it.

What we sell

  • Walk-in shower kits in curbed, low-threshold and barrier-free versions, across 34 stock footprints and 77 size-and-style configurations.
  • Shower pans on their own, with thresholds from 0.5″ to 2″ in quarter-inch steps, for bathrooms where the walls are still sound.
  • Wall panel systems in smooth, tile-look and stone-look finishes.
  • Custom sizes and colors built to order when no stock footprint fits.
  • Bestbath products, including RANE by Bestbath. Aging Safely Baths also sells Hydro Dimensions, Ella’s Bubbles (as an authorized dealer) and Meditub walk-in tubs, for households where a tub suits better than a shower.

How we work

Most people who call us have a measurement, a photo and a worry. We start with who will use the shower, then the room, then the product. A quote is free and in writing. Ask us to spell out what ships, what does not, and what freight costs to your ZIP code, and we will. If you want the shower installed, we quote that separately so you can see the product and the labor as two numbers. If you are doing the work yourself or have your own contractor, we ship supply-only.

We will tell you when a different product makes more sense, including when the right answer is a pan on its own rather than a full kit, or a walk-in tub rather than a shower.

How the guides on this site are written

The articles here are written by the people who answer the phone, from the questions we get asked most. Where we quote a national price range, a building code or a safety figure, we link the source so you can read it yourself. Where something depends on your house, your city or the specific product, we say so rather than guess. Prices on our own products are reviewed periodically and can change with materials and freight; your written quote is the number that counts.

What we do not do

  • We do not publish reviews or testimonials we cannot show you the source of.
  • We do not run countdown timers or “today only” prices.
  • We do not sell your details. See our privacy page.
  • We do not promise warranty terms we have not shown you in writing. See our warranty page.
